# Tools & Libraries Added

**Date:** 26 July 2026

## Audio & Speech Stack

### SpeechRecognition

**Purpose:**
Provides speech-to-text interfaces and supports multiple recognition backends.

**Current Status:**
Installed.

**Future Use:**
May be used as a fallback speech recognition engine.

---

### sounddevice

**Purpose:**
Records audio directly from the system microphone.

**Current Status:**
Installed and tested successfully.

**Verified:**

* Microphone access
* Audio recording
* Stable capture

---

### SciPy

**Purpose:**
Converts recorded audio into standard WAV files.

**Current Status:**
Installed.

**Current Use:**
Saving microphone recordings as `temp.wav`.

---

### NumPy

**Purpose:**
Stores and processes recorded audio samples efficiently.

**Current Status:**
Installed.

**Current Use:**
Audio buffering and numerical processing.

---

### OpenAI Whisper

**Purpose:**
Local AI speech-to-text model.

**Current Status:**
Installed successfully.

**Future Use:**
Convert recorded speech into text for Friday.

**Advantages:**

* Offline speech recognition
* High accuracy
* Replaceable with newer speech models in the future

---

### PyTorch

**Purpose:**
Machine learning framework used to run Whisper.

**Current Status:**
Installed automatically with Whisper.

**Future Use:**
Will also support future local AI models if required.

---
